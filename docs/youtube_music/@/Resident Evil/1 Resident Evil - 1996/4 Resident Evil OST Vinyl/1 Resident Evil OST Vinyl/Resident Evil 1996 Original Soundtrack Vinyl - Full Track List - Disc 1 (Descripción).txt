Music is by the Capcom Sound Team and composed by Koichi Hiroki, Makoto Tomozawa, Masami Ueda and Ludwig van Beethoven (The Moonlight Sonata).

The vinyl's themselves have a grey + red colour (Brain Matter) effect and are 180g heavyweight LPs.

In partnership with Capcom, Laced Records have released this triple vinyl set for the game that birthed the term “survival horror”, Resident Evil aka Bio Hazard in Japan.  

The Laced team conducted extensive research into track sources; multiple, often conflicting official and fan titles; and track lengths, factoring in things like music loops and gameplay events. Meanwhile, sleeve artist Boris Moncel of @BlackmaneDesign beavered away on terrifying original pieces for this historic release, studying reference points for creatures and locations from a nearly 30-year-old game.

Thid Vinyl disc includes the iconic original 1996 soundtrack by Koichi Hiroki, Makoto Tomozawa and Masami Ueda and Ludwig van Beethoven for the The Moonlight Sonata. 45 tracks have been edited for length, and mastered specially for vinyl.
